Engineers Diary – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

KS Engineering & Construction provides Engineers Diary (Android package: com.ksenc.engineersdiary). This policy describes the intended privacy practices for the initial offline release.

Information collection and sharing

The initial release is designed to operate without accounts, advertising, analytics or transmission of personal information to the developer. Calculation inputs, saved results and any notes are processed locally on the device. We do not sell or share app user data.

Local storage and deletion

Users can remove locally saved information using available in-app deletion controls or by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Device or operating system backups, if enabled, may retain copies according to the device provider's settings.

Permissions and sharing

Any optional device permissions must be used only for the feature requested by the user. If users choose to export or share results, the receiving application or service may process that information under its own privacy policy.

Third-party services

Google Play and the device operating system may independently process information under their own privacy policies. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information under the GitHub privacy statement.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for engineering and professional use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes

We will update this policy before introducing features that materially change our privacy practices.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact KS Engineering & Construction at admin@ksenc.in.
